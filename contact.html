Frequently Asked Questions

Admissions & Enrollment FAQs

Find immediate answers to questions on eligibility, cutoffs, merit scholarships, and campus facilities.

What are the entrance exams accepted for B.Tech and MBA at education.in?
For B.Tech degrees, we accept JEE Main percentiles, state CET scores, or our autonomous education.in Scholastic Aptitude Test (ESAT). For MBA programs, candidates with valid CAT, GMAT, GRE, or XAT percentiles are eligible for direct personal interview rounds without taking the written test.
How does the Merit Scholarship selection process work?
education.in awards over ₹25 Crores annually in merit grants. Students securing 90%+ in prior board examinations or top percentiles in entrance tests automatically qualify for 30% to 50% tuition waivers. Scholarships are renewed each academic year contingent upon maintaining a minimum 7.5 CGPA with clean disciplinary standing.
Are on-campus hostel and residential accommodations available?
Yes. Our Bengaluru and Gurugram campuses feature air-conditioned single, twin, and studio residences with high-speed Wi-Fi, 24/7 security with biometric access, multi-cuisine hygienic dining halls, on-campus health clinics, gymnasium, and indoor sports centers.
Do you offer global dual-degrees and credit transfer to overseas universities?
Yes. We have established institutional agreements with 180+ global universities across the USA, UK, Germany, and Singapore. Under our 2+2 or 3+1 dual-degree pathways, students can complete their final semesters abroad and earn internationally recognized dual credentials.
Can international and NRI students apply? Is visa assistance provided?
We welcome students from over 40 countries. Our dedicated International Student Office (ISO) manages foreign transcript equivalencies, issues statutory Bonafide admission letters for Student Visa (FRRO) registration, and provides dedicated arrival support.
